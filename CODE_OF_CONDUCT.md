# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of MetaAMR-Plus pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- Harassment, trolling, or discriminatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behaviour and are expected to take appropriate corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behaviour.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a GitHub issue at https://github.com/Clinical-Genomics-Linkoping/metaAMR-Plus/issues or contacting the maintainers directly at https://github.com/Leilanasd or https://github.com/JD2112.
